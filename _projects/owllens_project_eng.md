### Privacy First
- **Fully offline**: Built-in AI models, no internet required
- **Local processing**: All operations are performed locally to ensure data security
- **No cloud storage**: Your photos always stay on your own device

### Flexible Import
- **Multiple import methods**: Supports importing from folders
- **System album integration**: Seamlessly connects to your system photo library
- **Batch processing**: Easily manage large numbers of images
